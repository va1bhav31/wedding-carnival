How the show runs

Three steps. You only do the first one.

  1. 1

    You tell us, we build it

    Send your names, photos, colours and the stories behind your trivia. We set up your own branded game hub on its own link.

  2. 2

    Guests scan, then play

    One QR code on the tables. Guests join with just a name, on any phone. No app to download.

  3. 3

    Your host runs the show

    Games go live from the host’s phone at the right moment, and everyone watches the leaderboard move as they play.
